He was explicit that he sourced all three tools to trial himself; neither of us proactively surfaced this as a solved problem beforehand. He wants the report to say plainly that the "collective wisdom" didn't work for this project, and that our own approach is offered because it might be useful to someone else hitting the same wall.

Concretely:

- New title and opening thesis lead with the negative finding across all three tools, stated once, plainly, before any trial detail.
- Section 1 now flags explicitly that capability 2 (cross-linking) turned out to already be solved before any tool was tested — that's not incidental background anymore, it's evidence for the thesis.
