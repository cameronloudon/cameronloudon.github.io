## Thread 1 — Closing the remaining architecture-map open items with Cowork

**What happened:** Following up on the merged architecture map, sent Cowork a request to resolve or explicitly status-lock every remaining open item so the third-party auditor version could be finalized. Cowork's response resolved five items:

1. **`draft-agent-config.md` → `_ai-context/draft-agent-config.md`** — Cowork sent the current content and flagged that it was still missing the check-and-balance workflow and PowerShell-prompt convention (Findings #14/#15 from its own 2026-06-29 audit), leaving the call on whether to fix before committing to Claude Code. Added both sections before committing, since shipping a known gap into what becomes the permanent, audited copy would have been exactly the kind of undocumented deviation this whole effort has been trying to eliminate.

2. **`cowork-memory-backup.md`** — Cowork did not send the stale content as-is. It refreshed the file first, removing the dated "project state" section entirely (the section that had gone stale twice — 8 days, then 14 days) in favour of pointers to `PROJECT_STATE.md` and `_session-logs/`. This is a structural fix, not another refresh, and it directly resolves Open Decision #14: Cowork committed to sending updates whenever `cowork-conventions.md` or `draft-agent-config.md` changes, picked up at the next Phase 2 run rather than a new mechanism.

3. **Two missing permalinks** — `approach.html` and `master-synthesis-prompt.html` both lacked `permalink:` front matter, unlike every other page. Cowork had no objection and no knowledge of a deliberate reason. Fixed both (`/approach/`, `/master-synthesis-prompt/`), and updated the one internal link to the latter (in `approach.html`) to match.

4. **Folder-per-entry for `_signals/` and `_now/`** — Cowork pushed back on migrating either, with two arguments: `_signals/`'s flat-file pattern is legitimate and already in use (not an undocumented deviation the way the `_ideas/` files were), and `_now/`'s two entries were decided as flat files a few hours earlier through the full proposal → review → consensus process — revisiting the same day would relitigate a decision just made carefully. Cowork proposed narrowing the merged map's Rule 1 from "folder-per-entry, universally" to "folder-per-entry when an entry has or is likely to need sub-pages; flat-file remains valid for single-page entries with none in view." Accepted — this describes what all three collections are already doing correctly, rather than forcing a rule that didn't fit two of them.

5. **Root-level separation and `about/` restructuring** — statuses locked for the auditor version rather than decided. Root-level separation stays explicitly "proposed, not decided" (highest blast-radius item, no forcing function; Cowork was clear the auditor deadline isn't a reason to rush this). `about/` restructuring is "recommended, not yet actioned" — a real forcing function exists (`work-history.html`/`skills.html` already documented as planned), lower risk to do ahead of those pages being built, but timing remains Cameron's call.
