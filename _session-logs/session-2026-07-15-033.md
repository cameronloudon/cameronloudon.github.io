## Thread 1 — Archive Growth Chart Scaling: Design Discussion and Build

**What happened:** Cameron asked how the `/stats/` page's Archive Growth bar chart scales as its `growth_history` checkpoint count grows past the current 6 points. Traced the actual Liquid/CSS implementation: each bar is one calendar-day checkpoint, rendered with `flex: 1` (bars shrink to fit however many exist) and no cap, scroll, or downsampling — a real, previously unaddressed scaling gap. Proposed fixed-width bars plus horizontal scroll as a message to Cowork for independent review, per the Agent Participation Guide. Cameron then invited her to propose a genuinely different representation, not just react. She countered with a build-time SVG line chart (no JS at all), naming a real cost of the scroll approach (most of the archive's growth invisible by default, undercutting the page's own "receipts, not vanity metric" premise) against a real cost of her own alternative (per-point precision needs hover, no clean touch equivalent). Independently verified two of her claims directly (that `theme.js` is genuinely the only `.js` file on the site, and that `CLAUDE.md` literally states the single-JS-file convention) before responding. The two agents converged on the line chart, with Cowork supplying a concrete fix for Liquid's integer-truncation risk (force float division) and Claude Code adding a CSS-only hover-reveal refinement on top of her plain-text summary-line mitigation.

**Decisions made:** Cameron's own decision went the other way from where the two agents converged — bar chart plus scroll, on mobile-interaction grounds (horizontal scroll is a native, well-understood touch gesture; hover has no clean touchscreen equivalent, and reliable tap-to-reveal would need its own JS addition, a bigger ask than the scroll-position snippet). The line-chart design (float-division fix, hover-reveal structure, summary-line mitigation) was explicitly kept on record for a possible future revisit, not discarded. Built as decided: `stats/stats.html`'s `.stats-bar-wrap` changed from `flex: 1` to a fixed `flex: 0 0 60px`, `.stats-chart` gained `overflow-x: auto`, and a guarded snippet was added to `assets/js/theme.js` (inside the existing `DOMContentLoaded` listener, checking for `.stats-chart` before acting) that sets the chart's scroll position to its far right on load. Confirmed no conflicting rules exist in `main.css` for the changed selectors before treating this as page-scoped-only.

**Unresolved:** None. At the current 6 checkpoints the fix is a visual no-op (content still fits the container) — it only engages once history grows past what fits, which is the intended behavior.
