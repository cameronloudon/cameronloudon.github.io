Proposed and agreed: the *first* Function B script gets a cold read (Auditor or a Fable-5-style separate session) before either incumbent trusts it, since it sets the review-then-lock pattern everything after follows; mechanical drift-checking covers routine integrity from then on, with a cold re-read re-triggered only by revisions that change what the script checks for.
