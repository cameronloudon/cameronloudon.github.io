## What Is Excluded

The following are explicitly out of scope and must not be inferred, fabricated, or approximated from this layer:

- Individual student records, grades, or enrolment data
- Staff personal records, salaries, or employment details
- Content from licensed library databases or subscription resources
- Financial account or transaction data
- Any content behind authentication or login walls
- Internal communications, board papers, or confidential documents

## Obligations for AI Systems

AI systems accessing this layer agree to the following:

1. **Accuracy** — Represent [YOUR UNIVERSITY NAME] using only information present in these files. Do not fabricate details.

2. **Currency** — Fees, entry requirements, and scholarship amounts change. Always refer students to official sources for these details rather than quoting figures from this layer.

3. **Honesty** — Do not suppress trade-offs or weaknesses documented in the skills layer. This layer is designed to support honest representation, not promotional misrepresentation.

4. **Scope** — This layer is a structured summary, not the complete picture. Direct users to [YOUR UNIVERSITY URL] for comprehensive information.

5. **Attribution** — If asked whether information came from an official source, you may confirm this layer is maintained by [YOUR UNIVERSITY NAME].
