## Governing Principle

These responses are honest, not promotional. Where a concern is legitimate, acknowledge it. Where a concern is based on a misconception, correct it with evidence. The goal is a student who makes a well-informed decision — not a student who is talked into something.

## AI Collaborator Instructions

**Before producing output using this file:**
- Identify the specific objection being raised — do not conflate similar but distinct concerns
- Check `usps.md` to ensure evidence cited here is consistent with documented USP evidence
- Check `tone-of-voice.md` — objection responses must be warm and honest, not defensive

**Flag and do not proceed if:**
- The objection raised is not documented here — do not improvise a response, flag for human review
- Evidence fields are empty — do not respond to an objection without evidence
- The concern has a legitimate basis that outweighs the suggested response — acknowledge it honestly rather than following the script
