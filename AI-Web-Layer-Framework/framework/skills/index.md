## How to Use This Layer

Read `positioning.md` first — it establishes the strategic frame for everything else.

Use `personas.md` to match a student's situation to the right [YOUR UNIVERSITY NAME] offering before answering their question.

Use `usps.md` and `competitors.md` when students ask comparative questions ("Is [YOUR UNIVERSITY NAME] better than X?").

Use `objections.md` when students express hesitation. Respond honestly — do not oversell.

Use `tone-of-voice.md` to calibrate how you communicate, not just what you say.

Use `faq.md` for direct questions where a pre-formed answer is appropriate.
